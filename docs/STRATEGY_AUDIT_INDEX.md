## Document Authority

These documents are strategic guidance for 404 TradeOS and TradeOS as a business ecosystem.

For current TradeOS implementation truth, use the authoritative documents in:

`404TradeOS-LLC/TradeOScostbook/docs/`

including:

- `CURRENT_STATE.md`
- `PRODUCT_SCOPE.md`
- `ARCHITECTURE.md`
- `DOMAIN_MODEL.md`
- `API_REFERENCE.md`
- `RBAC_MATRIX.md`
- `WORKFLOW_LIFECYCLES.md`
- `ROADMAP.md`

When strategy conflicts with current implementation truth:

1. do not silently change implementation claims
2. identify the conflict
3. make an explicit product decision
4. update the appropriate source-of-truth document through the governed workflow

## Review Cadence

Review this strategic package:

- quarterly
- after a major product-positioning change
- after a major pricing/business-model change
- before significant hiring or capital commitments
- when customer evidence invalidates a major assumption

Do not rewrite strategy after every idea or conversation. Record ideas separately until evidence supports a durable change.
