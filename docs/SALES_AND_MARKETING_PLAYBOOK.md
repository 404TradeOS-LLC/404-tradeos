# 404 TradeOS Sales and Marketing Playbook

## Purpose

Turn the 404 TradeOS brand, website, services, and TradeOS product into one coherent customer-acquisition system for trade contractors.

## Positioning

404 TradeOS is not a generic web agency. It is a contractor technology company that helps trade businesses get found, capture leads, follow up faster, win more work, and eventually run operations through TradeOS.

Primary market position:

> Growth systems and operating software built specifically for trade contractors.

## Ideal Customer Profile

Initial focus:

- 5–30 field technicians or equivalent crew capacity
- owner-led or small management team
- growing beyond spreadsheets, disconnected apps, and informal processes
- too operationally complex for entry-level tools
- not ready for ServiceTitan-level cost, complexity, implementation burden, or enterprise process
- values practical outcomes over technical terminology

Secondary segments:

- strong solo operators preparing to hire
- specialty contractors with high average ticket values
- agencies or consultants serving trades who could become channel partners

## Core Problems We Sell Against

- missed calls and slow lead response
- weak websites and poor local visibility
- inconsistent estimating and pricing
- scattered customer/job information
- administrative overload
- delayed proposals and invoices
- weak follow-up
- owner dependence for every decision
- inability to see margin, capacity, and risk early

## Core Promise

404 TradeOS helps contractors spend less time chasing paperwork and more time running profitable work.

TradeOS should support that promise by preparing work, surfacing exceptions, and asking for approval only where judgment is required.

## Lead Magnets

Priority order:

1. Contractor Digital Health Check
2. Missed-Revenue Calculator
3. Website and Local Visibility Scorecard
4. Estimate Turnaround Benchmark
5. Follow-Up Speed Audit
6. Contractor Software Readiness Assessment

Every lead magnet should produce an immediate useful result and capture structured qualification data.

## Recommended Primary Funnel

1. Visitor arrives from search, referral, social, or outreach.
2. Visitor sees a clear contractor-specific outcome.
3. Visitor completes a diagnostic or requests a review.
4. System generates a short preliminary report.
5. Lead is scored by trade, size, service area, urgency, and likely value.
6. Qualified lead receives a discovery invitation.
7. Discovery identifies revenue leakage and operational friction.
8. Proposal presents a phased system, not a list of disconnected services.
9. Customer enters an onboarding workflow with milestones and reporting.
10. Successful service customers receive TradeOS expansion offers.

## Discovery Framework

Ask about:

- trade and service mix
- service area
- team size and trucks/crews
- average job value
- current lead sources
- missed calls and response time
- estimate turnaround time
- close rate
- current software
- biggest administrative burden
- biggest growth constraint
- desired outcome over the next 12 months

Do not lead with features. Diagnose economic and operational impact first.

## Sales Narrative

### Problem

Contractors often buy separate tools for websites, leads, CRM, estimating, scheduling, and reporting. The owner still becomes the integration layer.

### Cost

That fragmentation causes missed work, delayed responses, inconsistent pricing, weak visibility, and too much owner involvement.

### Solution

404 TradeOS builds the growth system. TradeOS becomes the operational system. Both are designed for trade contractors and work toward the same outcome.

### Proof

Use live product demos, real workflows, verified performance data, and real contractor outcomes. Never fabricate logos, metrics, reviews, or case studies.

### Close

Recommend the smallest phase that creates measurable value and a natural expansion path.

## Demo Strategy

Every demo must answer:

1. What contractor problem is shown?
2. What work disappears or becomes easier?
3. What decision becomes faster or safer?
4. What financial outcome could improve?

Preferred demo progression:

- lead arrives
- follow-up is prepared
- customer/project record is organized
- estimate is assembled
- proposal is prepared
- work is scheduled
- invoice and follow-up are prepared
- owner sees only exceptions and approvals

## Regional Strategy

Start with a concentrated Midwest footprint where local credibility, in-person relationships, and contractor referrals can compound.

Sequence:

1. Terre Haute and surrounding region
2. Indianapolis and central Indiana
3. Illinois border markets and nearby Midwest metros
4. trade-specific regional expansion
5. national expansion after repeatable acquisition and onboarding are proven

1. Clarify the company/product relationship on the site.
2. Build one real diagnostic lead magnet.
3. Connect every interactive demo to an outcome and CTA.
4. Create one highly credible case study.
5. Define a repeatable discovery and proposal process.
6. Begin capturing baseline funnel metrics.

## Guardrails

- no fake scarcity
- no fabricated performance claims
- no broad national positioning before operational capacity exists
- no custom work that damages TradeOS core architecture
- no feature-first sales conversations
- no promising autonomous AI actions that are not safely implemented
