# 404 TradeOS Company Operating Model

## Purpose

Define how 404 TradeOS and TradeOS should operate as a disciplined company without creating unnecessary bureaucracy.

## Company Structure

### 404 TradeOS

Primary responsibilities:

- customer acquisition
- agency and implementation revenue
- brand and market development
- contractor consulting
- service delivery
- customer relationships
- funding early product growth

### TradeOS

Primary responsibilities:

- scalable recurring software revenue
- contractor operations platform
- product development
- platform reliability and security
- product onboarding and customer success
- long-term enterprise value

The businesses reinforce each other but must maintain separate economics, product priorities, and performance measures.

## Operating Principle

Keep the organization small, clear, and accountable. Add process only when it prevents repeated errors, protects customers, or increases throughput.

## Founder Role

The founder should progressively move through four stages:

1. Builder and seller
2. Product owner and lead relationship manager
3. Chief executive and market strategist
4. Capital allocator and culture steward

The company should systematically remove the founder as the required approval point for routine work while preserving founder control over product vision, major spending, brand, and high-risk decisions.

## Recommended Hiring Sequence

Hiring should follow proven bottlenecks, not titles.

Likely sequence:

1. Contract implementation/design capacity
2. Reliable product engineering support
3. Customer onboarding/support coordinator
4. Sales development or appointment-setting capacity
5. Customer success manager
6. QA/release engineering
7. Product manager
8. Additional specialized engineers
9. Marketing/content owner
10. Finance/operations leadership

Do not hire a large sales team before onboarding, product reliability, and customer outcomes are repeatable.

## Weekly Operating Cadence

### Monday: Executive Priorities

- revenue and cash position
- sales pipeline
- customer issues
- product/release status
- top three company priorities
- decisions required from founder

### Midweek: Product and Delivery Review

- active implementation work
- blockers
- scope changes
- customer commitments
- product feedback
- release risk

### Friday: Company Review

- wins and losses
- shipped outcomes
- customer health
- financial movement
- documentation and operational drift
- next-week priorities

Keep meetings short and decision-oriented.

## Monthly Review

Review:

- revenue by line of business
- recurring revenue
- gross margin
- cash runway
- lead sources and conversion
- delivery capacity
- product adoption
- support volume
- churn or cancellation risk
- highest-impact customer feedback
- technical and security risk

## Quarterly Planning

Each quarter should define:

- one company objective
- one revenue objective
- one product objective
- one customer-success objective
- one operational-strength objective

Anything not supporting those objectives is explicitly deprioritized.

## Decision Rights

### Founder Approval Required

- company positioning changes
- major product-scope changes
- pricing-model changes
- material spending commitments
- high-risk legal/security decisions
- hiring leadership roles
- acquisitions or partnerships with long commitments

### Delegated Decisions

- routine customer support
- approved roadmap implementation
- low-risk content and campaign execution
- standard onboarding
- normal bug fixes
- documented infrastructure operations

## Product Intake Rules

New ideas enter a structured intake, not the active roadmap.

Every idea must state:

- contractor problem
- target user
- expected business/user outcome
- evidence
- simplest possible intervention
- complexity and maintenance cost
- whether existing workflows can solve it

No feature receives priority solely because a competitor has it.

## AI Operating Model

AI should be treated as leveraged capability, not organizational memory.

Requirements:

- repository and approved documents remain source of truth
- agents follow startup and completion protocols
- material decisions are recorded
- outputs are reviewed according to risk
- no agent silently changes product scope
- human approval remains required for external or high-impact actions

## Financial Discipline

Separate reporting for:

- agency setup revenue
- agency recurring revenue
- custom implementation revenue
- TradeOS subscription revenue
- support/services revenue
- product development expense

Key rules:

- protect cash runway
- avoid staffing ahead of proof
- avoid underpricing custom work
- track contribution margin by offer
- use agency cash flow strategically, not automatically, to fund product work
- establish measurable go/no-go gates for major investments

## Customer Promise Management

All customer commitments must be recorded with:

- scope
- owner
- due date
- acceptance criteria
- dependencies
- pricing

Do not let verbal exceptions become permanent product obligations.

## Quality Gates

Before a customer-facing release:

- product behavior verified
- security and tenancy implications reviewed
- documentation updated
- rollback understood
- customer communication prepared
- support impact considered

## Company Health Signals

Healthy:

- customers achieve measurable outcomes quickly
- recurring revenue grows
- support burden per customer decreases
- implementation becomes repeatable
- roadmap is evidence-driven
- founder involvement shifts toward high-value decisions

Unhealthy:

- too many custom exceptions
- product promises exceed implementation
- revenue grows but cash declines
- support volume rises faster than customers
- agents or teams work from conflicting context
- founder remains required for routine execution

## Twelve-Month Organizational Goal

Create a repeatable system where:

- leads are qualified consistently
- sales follow a standard process
- onboarding has clear milestones
- product releases follow governance
- customer outcomes are reviewed
- financial performance is visible
- the founder is not carrying all context personally
